deadlock-api-ingest — passive Steam HTTP-cache discovery reference

Hideout adapts the cache-reference discovery approach from src/scan_cache.rs and
src/utils.rs. It does not bundle the Rust application or its saved-session/Game
Coordinator recovery functionality.
